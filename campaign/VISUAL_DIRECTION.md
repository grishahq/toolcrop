# ToolCrop visual direction

Use dark navy (#0c1720), mint (#7ce3ba), warm amber and restrained brass details. Concepts use tactile paper, parcels and transparent mechanical test rigs. Typography is readable English. The recurring idea is that an instruction card must survive a real process and reach the independently specified destination.

`assets/concept-filter.png` and `assets/concept-destination.png` are original AI-generated conceptual artwork produced with the built-in image_gen tool. They are metaphors, never software screenshots or evidence charts. Exact prompts and source provenance are recorded in concept-prompts.json and concept2-prompt.json; both were visually inspected for readable text and absence of metric/adoption claims.

`assets/real-report-screenshot.jpg` is the real generated HTML report, captured through the browser at 1280×720. `assets/filter-comparison.png` is a Matplotlib plot from evidence/inventory-v1/report.json; scripts/plot_evidence.py is its reproducible source. Keep the synthetic-fixture disclosure visible when sharing or cropping. Do not decorate these assets with unmeasured quality, speed, cost, adoption or hiring outcomes.

Default use: concept-filter for problem framing; concept-destination for design tradeoff; screenshot for demo and release; measured chart for the fixed corruption experiment. Reuse original assets consistently; no need to generate a different visual for every platform.
